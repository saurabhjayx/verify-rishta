# How to Identify Matrimonial Fraud in India: Warning Signs and Practical Steps

Most relationship decisions involve uncertainty, and a mismatch or awkward conversation does not automatically mean fraud. Still, certain patterns deserve attention, especially when someone pressures you for money, provides inconsistent material information, or asks you to keep a financial transaction secret. Focus on observable behaviour and reliable evidence rather than stereotypes or rumours.

## Warning signs to take seriously

Be cautious if someone:

- repeatedly changes important facts about identity, employment or marital status;
- asks for urgent transfers or loans before you have independently understood the situation;
- sends documents that cannot be reasonably explained;
- pressures you to bypass normal checks;
- insists on secrecy around money;
- uses threats and emotional pressure to force a decision.

One sign alone may have an innocent explanation, but a pattern can justify slowing down.

## Verify before making commitments

Do not send money simply because of an emotional story or an approaching wedding date. Independently confirm payment requests through a trusted channel. Review material claims with consent and authorised sources.

:::callout{type="warning"}
Be wary of links asking for passwords, one-time codes, bank details or identity documents. Never let urgency replace basic checks.
:::

## If you suspect a scam

Pause payments and avoid confronting the person in a way that could put you at risk. Save relevant messages, transaction references, profile details and documents in their original form. Do not edit evidence or publish accusations online.

Contact your bank or payment provider quickly if money has been transferred. Report online financial fraud on the [National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal](https://cybercrime.gov.in/) or call the cyber crime helpline on 1930. If there is immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

## Distinguish red flags from proof

A late reply, job change, family disagreement, financial difficulty or an inconsistent online profile does not establish fraud by itself. Verify the underlying fact, give a fair opportunity to clarify, and seek qualified advice when the stakes are high. A complaint, allegation or investigation should not be described as a conviction or proven wrongdoing.

## How verification can help

A consent-based verification process can help check selected factual claims before a decision, but it cannot guarantee that fraud will never occur. Ask any provider to define its checks, sources, identity-matching process and limitations. Treat the result as one part of a broader decision that includes communication, boundaries and judgment.

## What the law says

The provisions below are summarised from the official text on India Code. Laws and their commencement change, so check the current text or ask a lawyer before you rely on them.

- [Section 318 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023](https://indiacode.gov.in/act/4bb3e636-ed67-4731-8fb1-7ebfd83e1584/sections) defines cheating, and [Section 319](https://indiacode.gov.in/act/4bb3e636-ed67-4731-8fb1-7ebfd83e1584/sections) covers cheating by personation, which means pretending to be someone else.
- [Section 69 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023](https://indiacode.gov.in/act/4bb3e636-ed67-4731-8fb1-7ebfd83e1584/sections) covers sexual intercourse obtained by deceitful means or a false promise to marry, and its explanation includes marrying by suppressing identity.
- Under [Section 82 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023](https://indiacode.gov.in/act/4bb3e636-ed67-4731-8fb1-7ebfd83e1584/sections), marrying again while a husband or wife is living, where the new marriage is void for that reason, is an offence.
- [Section 3 of the Dowry Prohibition Act, 1961](https://indiacode.gov.in/act/8dc8e1b4-7147-45ea-9e82-5911aa3816d4/sections) penalises giving or taking dowry, and [Section 4](https://indiacode.gov.in/act/8dc8e1b4-7147-45ea-9e82-5911aa3816d4/sections) penalises demanding it, directly or indirectly, from the family of the bride or groom.

## The bottom line

Protecting yourself does not require assuming the worst about every prospective partner. Slow down when pressured, verify material claims responsibly, protect your information, and use official channels if you believe a crime has occurred. The aim is informed caution, not fear.
